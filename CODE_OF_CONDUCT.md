# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

* Be respectful and welcoming to people of every background and experience level.
* Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise ideas and code, not people.
* Be honest about results. Do not promote this project as a way to guaranteed profit, and do not
  use the project's spaces to sell signals, accounts, paid "versions" or investment schemes.
* No harassment, insults, discrimination, spam, or publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules,
and may block contributors who repeatedly or seriously break them. Report problems privately to the
repository owner through GitHub (see the owner's profile for contact options).
